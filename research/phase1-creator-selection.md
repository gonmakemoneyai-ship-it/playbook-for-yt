# Phase 1 — Selecting the Five Educators

## Method

Candidates were evaluated against the 15 criteria in the brief, with heaviest weight on (1) demonstrated real-world results on channels other than their own, (2) depth/specificity vs. generic tip-recycling, and (3) whether elite professional creators actually use or cite their work.

## Final five

1. **Paddy Galloway** — data/outlier-analysis strategist, MrBeast-credited
2. **Derral Eves** — longest-tenured consultant, MrBeast/T-Series-scale experience, author of *The YouTube Formula*
3. **Colin & Samir** — insider-interview journalists who extract and pressure-test top operators' real methodology
4. **Jenny Hoyos** — practitioner who documents her own MrBeast-style testing process transparently
5. **Tim Schmoyer (Video Creators)** — analytics-literacy specialist, uniquely brings YouTube's own staff on to explain platform mechanics

Together these five cover: (a) pure data/outlier methodology, (b) large-scale cross-channel consulting patterns, (c) insider/insider-team accounts of elite operations including MrBeast's, (d) a transparent practitioner's real-time testing process, and (e) platform-fact grounding from YouTube's own staff. This combination avoids relying on any single voice's blind spots and avoids the generic-tips tier the brief explicitly rules out.
