# playbook-for-yt

A universal, evidence-based YouTube growth playbook, built by researching five vetted YouTube growth educators and cross-referencing their teachings against each other and against confirmed platform statements from YouTube's own staff (CEO Neal Mohan, Global Head of Culture & Trends Kevin Allocca, and Director of Product Management Tom Leung).

## Start here

**[`PLAYBOOK.md`](./PLAYBOOK.md)** is the main deliverable — a self-contained operating system meant to be handed, in full, to another AI conversation alongside real YouTube Analytics data. It covers channel positioning, idea generation/scoring, packaging, titles, thumbnails, hooks, retention, storytelling, analytics diagnostics, failure/winner analysis, competitor research, Shorts, and more, plus decision trees and AI operating instructions for combining this playbook with real data.

**Read the "mandatory disclosure" section near the top of `PLAYBOOK.md` first** — it explains an important limitation in how this research was gathered (no direct transcript access was possible in this environment; see below).

## Research evidence base

The `/research` folder contains the full research trail behind the playbook:

- `phase1-creator-selection.md` — the candidate pool comparison and why these five creators were selected.
- `paddy-galloway.md`, `derral-eves.md`, `colin-and-samir.md`, `jenny-hoyos.md`, `tim-schmoyer.md` — one dossier per creator, each covering their 5 selected sources (25 total) with metadata, transcript-availability notes, and individually tagged principles (FACT/PLATFORM INFORMATION vs. EXPERT OPINION vs. HYPOTHESIS/CREATIVE PRINCIPLE).
- `phase5-cross-reference.md` — where the five creators agree, partially agree, disagree, and where common advice turns out to be outdated or a myth given this evidence base.

## Important limitation

This research environment's `WebFetch` tool was blocked at the network level for every domain tested (YouTube itself, every transcript-extraction tool tried, and neutral controls like Wikipedia). Only web search remained available. As a result, **none of the 25 sources have a verified line-by-line transcript** — every one is explicitly marked `TRANSCRIPT NOT FULLY AVAILABLE` in its dossier, and all extracted principles were built from search-engine-surfaced quotations and reputable secondary coverage rather than primary transcripts. Nothing was fabricated; every dossier documents exactly what substitute material was used and flags any gaps. See each dossier's own access note for full detail.
